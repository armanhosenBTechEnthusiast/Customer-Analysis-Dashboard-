# 📊 Project Overview

The **Customer Analysis Dashboard** is a visually compelling and fully interactive Tableau project built to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, revenue distribution, and regional business trends.

This dashboard transforms complex datasets into meaningful visual stories, enabling organizations to make smarter, faster, and data-driven decisions.

---

# 🔥 Dashboard Features

## 💰 Revenue Performance Analytics
- Total Revenue KPI Tracking
- Month-wise Revenue Trend Analysis
- Sales Growth Monitoring
- Revenue Distribution Insights

## 🌍 Geographic Intelligence
- Interactive State-wise Revenue Map
- Regional Revenue Share Analysis
- High-Performing Market Identification

## 👥 Customer Segmentation
- Age-wise Sales Distribution
- Gender-wise Revenue Analysis
- Category-based Dynamic Filtering

## 📉 Advanced Business Insights
- Quantity vs Discount Correlation
- Consumer Purchase Pattern Analysis
- Interactive Drill-down Exploration
